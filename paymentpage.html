Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Is the hourly rate fixed for all project types?
Yes. Whether it's a quick UI script or a full game, the rate is always $16/hour. The quote reflects the estimated hours for your specific scope.
Can I pay in installments?
Yes. For larger projects, payments can be spread across 3 to 5 months depending on project size and agreement. Each installment comes with a signed invoice.
What happens if scope changes mid-project?
Minor adjustments are handled as part of the project. Major changes that significantly differ from the original purchase order may incur an additional fee of around 10%.
Are refunds available?
Once development has started, refunds are not provided as custom digital work cannot be reversed. Partial refunds may be considered if minimal progress has been made, at our discretion.
When do I receive ownership of the work?
Full intellectual property is transferred to you upon final payment. After transfer, Quantum Digitals retains no claim over the delivered work.
Do you offer a DW member discount?
Yes. Members of DW receive a 15% discount across all projects. Simply mention your membership when reaching out for a quote.
