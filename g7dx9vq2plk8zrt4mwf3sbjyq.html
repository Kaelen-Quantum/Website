Development Phases

Phase 1 · April 2026
Showcase Release
  • Core terrain sculpting and ocean floor topology
  • Primary landmark zones roughed in
  • Atmosphere, lighting and underwater fog
  • Surface water visuals including foam and caustics
  • Key biome areas defined across the map
  • Hero zone polished for public demo
Phase 2 · July 2026
Full Map Release
  • All zones fully built and detailed
  • Build kit assets placed throughout
  • Optimisation passes including LOD and streaming
  • Lore, signage and environmental storytelling
  • Final QA and playtesting sessions
  • Public launch
