Internal Policies

Article 1
Assets Protection
Any screenshots, media, or content that reveal or showcase aspects of your project will never be shared, reused, or published without your explicit consent. This includes portfolio use, promotional materials, or internal showcases. Your project and all related information remain strictly confidential at all times. We do not disclose any details about your project's structure, features, or implementation. Even in the event of a dispute or disagreement, we remain committed to upholding a respectful, secure, and confidential environment.
Article 2
Communication
We maintain clear and professional communication at every stage of the project. Our team typically responds within 24 to 48 business hours. Delays in client responses may impact the delivery timeline. Development work is paused during weekends and public holidays, in accordance with company policy.
Article 3
Modifications
If an asset or system has already been fully developed, any major modifications that significantly differ from what was outlined in the original purchase order may incur additional costs, typically around 10%.
Article 4
Payment Terms
All projects require a 50% upfront deposit to secure scheduling and begin development. The remaining balance is due upon completion, prior to final delivery. Payments must be made via authorized methods specified in the invoice or purchase order.
Article 5
Ownership Transfer
Full ownership of all assets and systems is transferred to you only after full payment has been received. Until that point, the rights remain the property of Quantum Digitals. After transfer, we no longer retain any claim or control over the delivered work.
Article 6
Refund Policy
As this service involves custom digital development, the European right of withdrawal (14 days) does not apply once work has begun. Refunds are therefore not provided after project initiation. Partial refunds may be considered only if minimal progress has been made, at the company's discretion.
Article 7
Post-Delivery Support
We offer limited support for up to 14 days after project delivery to address minor bugs or clarify system use. Any further development, updates, or feature additions require a new agreement.
Article 8
Security Compliance
We operate in alignment with GDPR and Roblox's platform policies. If you require a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA), we are happy to provide and sign one to ensure mutual confidentiality.
